The app

Your Mac, on every phone

A menu-bar app that turns whatever's playing on your Mac into the room's sound system.

  • 🎧
    Broadcast your audio — system sound, a DJ set, or a mic, out over the Wi-Fi.
  • 🕰️
    The whole room in sync — everyone drops together, a fraction of a second apart.
  • 🔒
    Plays when phones are locked — the music keeps going in every pocket, screen off.
  • 📱
    Guests scan a QR — no app, no login. iPhone and Android alike.
