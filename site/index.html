Questions? Answers.

What can I play?

Anything your Mac can play. Apple Music, a browser tab, or DJ software like rekordbox all work the same way, because PartyParty broadcasts the Mac’s own audio. Decks, a controller, or a turntable work too when they’re routed into your Mac through a mixer or audio interface.

Do my friends need to install PartyParty?

No. PartyParty is a Mac app for whoever is playing the music. Your friends scan a QR code and the party opens in their phone’s browser. No app, no account, no sign-up. It plays through the headphones they already have.

Do we need Wi-Fi or internet?

You need a Wi-Fi network that your Mac and your friends’ phones can all join. It doesn’t need to reach the internet: a travel router with no uplink works, so the music plays at a beach or anywhere else off the grid. An internet connection is needed the first time you set PartyParty up, and for recognizing tracks with Shazam.

How close is playback to the Mac?

Playback stays close to the live room and uses a short cushion for ordinary Wi-Fi hiccups. During the public beta, phones using the local and cloud-relayed paths can land at different delays. Test the exact network and devices before an important event.

How do I get PartyParty?

Through TestFlight, Apple’s beta service. Install TestFlight from the Mac App Store, then open the join link and press Install. Updates arrive the same way.

Which Macs and phones are supported?

The public beta runs on Apple-silicon Macs with macOS 26 or later. Guests join from Safari on a recent iPhone, with any headphones they like.

What does PartyParty cost?

The public beta is free for the DJ and everyone listening. That describes the beta, not a promise about future pricing.
