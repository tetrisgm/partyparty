The hard part, solved

Plays even when the phone is locked.

Most "stream to phones" tricks die the moment the screen turns off. partyparty is built on the same streaming tech as broadcast radio and TV, so the sound keeps going in every pocket — guests put their phone away and just listen.

🔒🎶
Screen off. Music on. No open tab required, no app in the foreground.
How guests join

Three steps, thirty seconds.

Hit Go Live

One green button in your Mac's menu bar. partyparty picks up your audio and starts broadcasting on the local Wi-Fi.

Guests scan the QR

A code and a short link appear. Everyone points their camera at it — straight into their browser, nothing to download.

Sound in their pocket

They tap play, lock their phone, and the whole room hears the same thing at the same time.

Under the hood

Built like a proper Mac app.

One self-contained menu-bar app — nothing for you to install and configure. Everything's embedded and code-signed.

The app
Swift menu-bar shell wrapping a single Go server binary. Zero dependencies — ffmpeg, the streaming server, and the capture helper are all bundled inside.
Capture
Apple ScreenCaptureKit for system audio, or AVFoundation for a mic / audio interface.
Encode
ffmpeg → AAC via Apple's AudioToolbox encoder.
Deliver
HLS / Low-Latency HLS — the only web audio transport that survives a locked phone. (WebRTC & Web Audio don't, so they're out.)
Sync
Phones NTP-sync to the Mac's clock and report their measured delay; the server computes an outlier-robust target so the room lands together.
Distribution
Developer ID signed, hardened-runtime, notarized & stapled. Sparkle auto-updates. Built in CI.
