What it is

A Mac app, and a page for every event.

The app streams your sound to the room. The page holds the event details, the live stream, the guests' posts, and the replay afterwards.

The app

Streams your Mac to every phone

A menu-bar app for macOS. It captures your Mac's audio and sends it out over your local Wi-Fi — guests receive it in their browser.

  • 🎧
    Pick your source — system audio (Spotify, a set, a video) or a mic / audio interface.
  • 📱
    Guests scan a QR — the stream opens in their phone browser. No app, no account, iPhone or Android.
  • 🕰️
    Everyone's in sync — each phone matches your Mac's clock, so the room hears the same moment — no echo.
  • 🔒
    Keeps playing when locked — pocket the phone, screen off, and the audio continues.
The event page

A web page for every event

Each set gets a shareable page — a link (and QR) that works before, during, and after the night.

  • 📅
    Post the details — date, place, lineup — then share the link so people know what's coming.
  • 🔴
    The live stream plays on it — guests listen right on the page while you're on.
  • 💬
    Guests post to the wall — comments, photos and videos, during or after. You approve what shows.
  • ⏺️
    The set is saved as a replay — when you stop, the whole stream uploads to the page to play back later.
  • ➕
    Follow + socials — followers get your next event; your SoundCloud, Instagram & Spotify are linked.

Why it sounds right

In sync — and it survives a locked screen.

Two hard problems, handled. Every phone matches your Mac's clock and the server keeps them aligned, so the room hears the same instant instead of a scattered echo. And it streams the way internet radio does (HLS) — the one method that keeps playing when a phone browser is in the background or the screen is off.

🎚️
Go live in one tap

A menu-bar button captures your chosen source and starts broadcasting on the Wi-Fi.

👀
See the room

A live list of who's connected and each phone's delay, so you can spot anyone lagging.

🎛️
Tune it

Set the audio quality and latency for the venue and the network you're on.

Step by step

What actually happens.

Open the app, Go Live

Pick your source — system audio or a mic. partyparty starts broadcasting on your Wi-Fi and shows a QR.

Guests scan the QR

The stream opens in their phone browser. No app, no account — iPhone or Android.

The room plays in sync

Every phone is time-aligned to your Mac, and keeps playing when the screen locks.

It's saved to the page

Stop the stream and the full set becomes a replay on the event page, alongside the crowd's posts.

Under the hood

Built like a proper Mac app.

One self-contained menu-bar app — a Swift shell around a single Go server, with ffmpeg and the streaming engine embedded. Nothing to configure.
