The sound

Everyone hears it.
At the same moment.

Whatever's on your Mac — a DJ set, Spotify, a mic — goes out to every phone over the Wi-Fi. The whole room drops together, a fraction of a second apart, instead of a chaotic echo.

🔒
Plays even when the phone is locked

Built on broadcast-grade streaming, so the music keeps going in every pocket — screen off, no tab open.

📱
Guests just scan a QR

No app to install, iPhone and Android alike. Optional sign-in to post; otherwise they listen as a guest.

🎚️
You stay in control

Pick the source, watch who's connected and each person's delay, tune quality for the room.

How it works

Three steps, thirty seconds.

Hit Go Live

One green button in your Mac's menu bar picks up your audio and starts broadcasting on the Wi-Fi.

Guests scan the QR

A code + short link appear. Everyone points a camera — straight into their browser, nothing to download.

Sound in every pocket

They tap play, lock their phone, and post to the wall as the night unfolds.

Under the hood

Built like a proper Mac app.

One self-contained menu-bar app — a Swift shell around a single Go server, with ffmpeg and the streaming engine embedded. Nothing to configure.
