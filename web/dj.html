What should I pick?

① Capture everything on the Mac (easiest)

Pick 🖥️ Capture the Mac’s output and press Go live. The first time, macOS asks to record system audio - click Allow, that’s it. No driver, no routing - it grabs whatever is playing (RekordBox, Spotify, etc.). Turn on Do Not Disturb so notification dings don’t go out.

② Hardware controller / mixer

Route the controller’s REC / USB record output to the Mac and select that device. (Pioneer: set a USB out to MIX/REC OUT.) Or run its REC/booth out into an audio interface input and pick the interface.

Keep everything at 48 kHz to avoid clicks.

If a guest still cannot connect

On a Wi-Fi with no working name lookup, guests can reach this Mac at its address directly. This link is plain HTTP and only works on this network.

How listening works

Guests point their camera at the QR code (or open the link) and the party opens in their browser. No app, no account.

The music

Your live stream plays on their phone a few seconds behind the booth - a fixed cushion that absorbs Wi-Fi hiccups - and keeps playing while the screen is locked.

The connection

On this Wi-Fi, phones connect straight to this Mac. If the venue's Wi-Fi keeps devices apart, guests take the internet relay automatically - same link, nothing to set up.

The party feed

While listening, guests can post photos and comments everyone sees. It is all live only - nothing is stored in the cloud.
