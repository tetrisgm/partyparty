Turn your Mac into a silent-disco rig.

Hit Go Live, hand out a QR, and your set plays in every guest’s own earbuds - in sync, low-latency, even with no internet. No app for them, no speakers, no noise complaint.

No password needed. PartyParty installs to your Applications folder (in your home folder) and opens when it’s done.

The first time you go live, macOS asks once to record system audio - just click Allow.
